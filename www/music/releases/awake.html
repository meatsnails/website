awake to the digital era - Protocol Gone

My first proper album
I find myself increasingly frustrated by the internet and digital tech it's quite saddening how much increasing encroachment it has in our lives, I want out of it.
All of these tracks were made in "Renoise" and I aimed for a dark harsh digital sound for these tracks the cover was made in GIMP I am very happy with how everything turned out
